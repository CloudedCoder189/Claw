Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

OpenClaw Personal Assistant is a private, owner-operated automation project. This policy explains how its Google integration is intended to access, use, store, share, and retain data. It is not a public application or a service offering public user accounts.

1. Operator and contact

The project is operated using a dedicated Google account. For privacy questions or requests concerning this integration, email openclaw189@gmail.com.

2. Google data that may be accessed

Google OAuth authorization enables the connected local tools to access data within the scopes granted by the owner. Depending on the specific workflow and granted permissions, this may include:

  • Gmail: Message content, senders, recipients, subjects, attachments, and message metadata as needed to find, summarize, draft, or manage email. Sending messages is intended to require owner approval.
  • Google Drive: File names, metadata, content, folder structures, and sharing permissions as needed to organize and deliver files.
  • Google Docs and Sheets: Document and spreadsheet content as needed to create, read, edit, or organize work products.
  • Google Calendar: Events, schedules, titles, descriptions, and invitee details where needed for authorized planning or calendar operations.

Authorization alone does not mean all these categories are continually read. The tools perform actions only when the owner invokes or configures a workflow. The app is not designed to collect unrelated Google account information.

3. Purposes of use

Google data is used solely to carry out the owner's requested or approved functions: creating and organizing reports, managing documents and spreadsheets, summarizing or drafting communications, checking schedules, delivering completed work, and maintaining the owner's automation workflows. It is not used for advertising, sold, or made available to data brokers.

4. Processing, third parties, and sharing

  • Google: Hosts the account's Gmail, Drive, Docs, Sheets, and Calendar data and processes authorized API calls.
  • Local tools: GOG CLI and Windows/OpenClaw automation process files and API responses on the operator's device. Local job files and logs may be created.
  • AI provider: When the owner chooses an AI-assisted workflow, relevant task text or selected Google content may be sent to the configured external AI provider (currently OpenAI) to generate a response or assist with a task. The owner should avoid submitting sensitive information unless appropriate. Provider processing follows its applicable terms and policies.
  • Discord: The assistant may send completion notifications, summaries, and links through a Discord bot. These messages are processed by Discord; the system should not include confidential source material in notifications.
  • Owner's other Google account: Completed files may be privately shared with an explicitly preapproved account. Files are not intentionally made publicly accessible by default.

Google user data is not sold and is not transferred for advertising or unrelated commercial purposes. Any Google user data made available to another service must be necessary for an authorized user-facing function and handled in accordance with applicable Google API Services User Data Policy requirements, including Limited Use restrictions where relevant.

5. Storage and security

OAuth credentials are managed by installed authentication tools rather than entered into this public website. Files and logs may reside in local Windows directories or isolated automation workspaces; finished deliverables may be uploaded to the dedicated Google Drive folder. Access is intended to be restricted by device permissions, OAuth grants, Google Drive sharing controls, and scoped automation permissions. No system can guarantee absolute security.

Important: This GitHub Pages website is informational. It does not collect Google passwords, OAuth tokens, or user files, and it does not run the assistant or directly call Google APIs.

6. Retention and deletion

Google-hosted files remain until the owner deletes them in Google Drive or other applicable Google services. Local reports, job manifests, and logs may remain until manually deleted or removed by configured cleanup procedures; no automatic deletion period is promised. The owner can remove local copies, delete shared files, and revoke OAuth access at any time. Copies already delivered to other authorized services may be subject to those services' retention settings.

7. User controls and revocation

The owner may review or revoke the app's Google permissions in Google Account connections, delete files in Google Drive, revoke individual sharing permissions, disable connected automation, or contact the operator at the address above. Revoking Google access stops future authorized API calls but does not automatically erase previously exported local files or messages already sent through Discord.

8. Public website and hosting

This information site is hosted with GitHub Pages. GitHub may process routine requests and access logs as described by its own privacy policies. No analytics, advertising trackers, contact forms, or cookies are intentionally added to these HTML pages. The public pages do not give visitors access to the assistant's Google account.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the project changes how it accesses or handles Google data. The update date above will be revised. Material changes to Google data uses should be disclosed and, where required, require updated authorization before those uses begin.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy or the project's use of Google data: openclaw189@gmail.com.
