Private, owner-operated automation

An AI workspace for research, organization, and deliverables.

OpenClaw Personal Assistant is a personal automation project operated for a single account owner. It coordinates approved research tasks, organizes work, and delivers completed files using connected services.

Personal-use project · Not open for public registration

What the assistant is designed to do

Research and reports

Research public sources, prepare structured findings, and create files for the owner's review.

Google Drive, Docs & Sheets

Store deliverables, create and organize documents or spreadsheets, and privately share approved outputs.

Gmail & Calendar

Support owner-authorized email and scheduling workflows. Sending messages or changing important information is intended to require approval.

Delivery notifications

Use a local automation workflow to upload completed reports and send the owner their links through Discord.

How Google access works

The owner authorizes access using Google's OAuth consent process. The installed GOG CLI and local automation tools use the approved permissions for Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs, and Sheets. Access is limited to workflows initiated or configured by the owner; connecting a service does not mean every available action is performed.

Private by design. Finished files are stored in the assistant account's Google Drive and shared only with the owner's preapproved account by default. The project does not offer public signup or sell Google user data. Local automation and, where the owner chooses to use them, external AI services and Discord may process task information. See the privacy policy for the details and limitations.
